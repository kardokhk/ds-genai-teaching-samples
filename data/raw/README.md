# Wine Quality data

`winequality-red.csv`, `winequality-white.csv` and `winequality.names` are the Wine Quality dataset from the UCI Machine Learning Repository (dataset 186), by Paulo Cortez, António Cerdeira, Fernando Almeida, Telmo Matos and José Reis (2009). They are redistributed unchanged under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0). Source, SHA-256 checksums and access date are in `MANIFEST.tsv`.

Citation: Cortez P, Cerdeira A, Almeida F, Matos T, Reis J. Modeling wine preferences by data mining from physicochemical properties. Decision Support Systems 47(4):547-553, 2009.

Only the red wines are used in the session; the white wines are the extension exercise for learners who finish early.
