# Annual and sickness leave policy 2026

This policy replaces version 4.0 (2024). It applies to all employees of Larkspur Freight Ltd (fictional) on permanent and fixed-term contracts from 1 January 2026.

## Purpose

The company wants people to rest, recover and plan time away without disrupting depot and customer operations. This policy sets out how much leave you receive, how to book it and how sickness absence is paid. Where this policy and your contract differ, your contract applies.

## Annual leave entitlement

The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Full-time employees with less than five years of continuous service receive 27 days of annual leave per leave year. Employees with five or more years of continuous service receive 30 days. These figures are in addition to public holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro rata entitlement based on contracted hours, rounded up to the nearest half day.

Employees who join or leave part way through the leave year receive a pro rata entitlement for the months worked. Payroll calculates any balance owed or overtaken in the final salary payment.

## Booking leave

Book all leave through the HR portal. Your line manager approves or declines each request within five working days. For any block of more than five consecutive working days, submit the request at least 14 calendar days before the first day of leave. Blocks of more than 10 consecutive working days also need approval from the Head of People. Depot teams publish a leave calendar each quarter so that no more than a quarter of any shift is away at once.

## Carrying leave over

You may carry up to 6 unused days into the next leave year. Carried days must be taken by 31 March or they are lost. Exceptions apply only where you could not take leave because of sickness or family leave; in that case HR agrees a revised deadline with you in writing.

## Buying extra leave

From 2026 you may buy up to 5 extra days of leave per leave year through salary sacrifice. Requests are accepted once a year, during the November window for the following leave year. The cost is spread across twelve monthly salary payments.

## Volunteering leave

Each employee may take 3 paid volunteering days per leave year for work with a registered charity or community group. Book volunteering days in the HR portal in the same way as annual leave. Unused volunteering days cannot be carried over.

## Sickness absence and pay

If you are unwell, tell your line manager before the start of your shift or working day. You may self-certify absence of up to 4 consecutive calendar days by completing the return-to-work form. For longer absences you must provide a fit note from a medical practitioner.

You receive full pay for the first 15 working days of sickness absence in any rolling twelve-month period. After that, statutory sick pay applies. Your line manager holds a short return-to-work conversation after every absence.

## Bereavement leave

Employees receive up to 7 days of paid bereavement leave on the death of a close family member, which includes a partner, child, parent or sibling. For other relatives, managers may grant up to two days of paid leave. Further time may be agreed as unpaid leave or annual leave.

## Family leave

Maternity, paternity, adoption and shared parental leave are covered by the separate Family Leave Policy, which is not part of this document.

## Records and review

HR keeps leave records for six years. The Head of People reviews this policy every two years, or sooner if the law changes.
