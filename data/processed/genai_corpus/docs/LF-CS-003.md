# Customer support service level agreement

This agreement sets the response and resolution targets that Larkspur Freight Ltd (fictional) commits to for customer support tickets. It applies to all customer accounts.

## Priority levels

P1 means freight is stopped or a shipment is lost. P2 means a delivery is late or a customs hold affects a shipment. P3 means a booking, document or invoice needs correcting. P4 means a general question or a request for information.

## Targets

First response is the time from ticket creation to the first reply from a named support agent. Resolution is the time to a fix or an agreed workaround.

| Priority | Plan | First response | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Standard | 45 minutes | 8 hours |
| P1 | Premium | 20 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 | Standard | 3 hours | 2 business days |
| P2 | Premium | 90 minutes | 1 business day |
| P3 | Standard | 1 business day | 5 business days |
| P3 | Premium | 6 hours | 3 business days |
| P4 | Standard | 3 business days | 15 business days |
| P4 | Premium | 1 business day | 10 business days |

The priority is set by the support agent when the ticket is triaged. Customers may ask for a different priority, giving the reason, and the Support Team Leader decides.

## Raising a ticket

Customers raise tickets in the customer portal or through their account manager. Each ticket should include the consignment reference, the collection or delivery date and a short description of the problem. Tickets without a consignment reference are accepted, but the clock for P3 and P4 targets starts once the reference is supplied.

## Support hours

Support is staffed from 07:00 to 20:00 UK time, Monday to Saturday. P1 tickets for Premium customers are handled at all hours, every day. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding UK public holidays.

## Service credits

If Larkspur misses a P1 first-response target, the customer receives a service credit of 5% of that month's support plan fee for each missed ticket. Service credits are capped at 25% of the monthly fee. Credits are applied to the next invoice and must be claimed within 60 days.

## Escalation

If a ticket misses 2 consecutive agreed updates, it is escalated to the Customer Operations Manager, who contacts the customer within one business day. Customers can also ask for escalation at any time through their account manager.

## Reporting

Larkspur sends each Premium customer a monthly report with ticket volumes, first-response and resolution performance by priority, and any service credits due. Standard customers receive the same report each quarter.

## Exclusions

Targets do not apply during events outside Larkspur's control, such as port closures, severe weather warnings or strikes at third-party terminals. Larkspur informs affected customers when an exclusion starts and ends.
