# Remote and hybrid working policy

This policy explains who may work away from a Larkspur site, how often, and what support the company provides. It applies to office-based roles only.

## Eligibility

Office-based roles in finance, people, commercial, customer operations and IT are hybrid roles. Warehouse, depot, driving and yard roles are site-based and are not eligible for remote working, because the work can only be done on site. Your offer letter states whether your role is hybrid.

## How much remote working

Employees in hybrid roles may work remotely for up to 3 days per week. Teams agree one shared office day each week so that colleagues meet in person. Remote days cannot be saved up and used in a later week.

All hybrid employees must be available during core hours, from 10:00 to 15:00 UK time, on every working day. Outside core hours you may arrange your working time flexibly with your manager.

## Equipment and allowances

The company provides a laptop, a headset and a monitor. New hybrid employees may claim a one-off home office allowance of 350 GBP for a desk, chair or lighting, with receipts, through the expenses system within three months of starting. Hybrid employees also receive a broadband contribution of 18 GBP per month, paid through payroll.

Company equipment remains company property. When you leave Larkspur, return all equipment within 10 working days of your last day. IT arranges a courier collection if you cannot bring it to a site.

## Working from another country

You may work from outside the United Kingdom for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Each request needs approval from the Head of People at least 30 days before travel, because tax and insurance rules differ between countries. Working abroad days can be combined with annual leave in the same trip. Work from countries under sanctions is never approved.

## Health, safety and data

Complete the home workstation self-assessment in the HR portal in your first month as a hybrid employee and again whenever you move home. Follow the IT security policy at home: lock your screen when you step away, use the company VPN for internal systems and do not print confidential documents at home.

## Changes to arrangements

Managers may ask hybrid employees to attend a site on additional days for training, audits or peak periods, giving at least two working days of notice where possible. Employees who want a permanent change to their pattern should make a flexible working request.
