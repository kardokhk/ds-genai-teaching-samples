# Supplier contract summary: Northbeam Haulage

This summary describes the subcontracted trunking agreement between Larkspur Freight Ltd (fictional) and Northbeam Haulage (fictional). It is a guide for operations and finance staff; the signed contract prevails if the two differ.

## Scope and term

Northbeam Haulage (fictional) provides overnight trunk runs between the Felixstowe depot and the Midlands hub with its own tractor units and drivers. The contract runs for three years, from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2028. Either party may end the contract early with 90 days of written notice.

## Price and payment

Larkspur pays 412 GBP per completed trunk run, excluding VAT. A fuel indexation adjustment is reviewed quarterly against a published diesel index, up or down. Invoices are paid 45 days after the end of the month in which the runs took place.

## Performance

The on-time target is 96.5% of runs arriving within 30 minutes of the booked time, measured monthly. For each full percentage point below target, Larkspur deducts 2% of that month's invoice, up to a maximum deduction of 10%. Performance is reviewed at a monthly meeting between the Procurement Manager and Northbeam's account lead.

## Insurance and liability

Northbeam holds goods-in-transit insurance of at least 2.5 million GBP per vehicle and provides a certificate every year. Damage and loss claims follow the standard conditions of carriage named in the contract.

## Operational requirements

Each trailer leaves the depot with a numbered security seal, recorded on the run sheet at departure and checked on arrival. Northbeam vehicles report their position to the Larkspur transport system through a telematics feed at least every five minutes. Drivers follow the site rules at both ends, including the speed limit in the yard and the requirement to stay in the driver lounge during loading. Northbeam must tell the Larkspur night planner at once if a run will be more than 30 minutes late.

## Subcontracting and staff

Northbeam may not pass runs to another haulier without written consent from the Procurement Manager. Northbeam is responsible for drivers' hours, licences and training records and shares a summary of these at the monthly review.

## Data and confidentiality

Shipment data that Northbeam receives through the telematics feed or run sheets is used only to perform the contract. Northbeam deletes it at the end of the contract, except where the law requires it to be kept.

## Contract owner

The Procurement Manager owns this contract. Send questions about rates and invoices to the Finance Controller.
