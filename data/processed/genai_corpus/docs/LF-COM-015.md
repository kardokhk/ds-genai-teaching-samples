## Surcharges and discounts

A fuel surcharge of 6.5% applies to all transport services and is reviewed each month. Storage, pick and pack and support plans are not subject to the fuel surcharge. Customers who book more than 50 pallets in a calendar month receive a volume discount of 4% on pallet services for that month.

## Terms

The minimum order value is 95 GBP per booking. Quotes are valid for 30 days from the date of issue. Prices for dangerous goods, oversized loads and temperature-controlled freight are quoted individually by the commercial team. Customs declarations cover up to five commodity lines; each further line is charged as a separate declaration.

## Support plans

Every account includes the Standard support plan unless the customer buys the Premium support plan. Response and resolution targets for each plan are set out in the customer support service level agreement.
