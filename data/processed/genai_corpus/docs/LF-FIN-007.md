# Travel and expense policy

This policy covers business travel and the expenses that Larkspur Freight Ltd (fictional) reimburses. It applies to all employees and to contractors whose contract says so.

## Before you travel

Book travel through the company travel desk in the expenses system. Your line manager approves every trip before booking. Choose the lowest reasonable fare that fits the business need.

## Rail and air

Use standard class rail for journeys of up to 3 hours. First class is allowed for single rail journeys longer than 3 hours. Book economy for flights; premium economy is allowed for flights with a scheduled duration of more than 7 hours. Business class is never reimbursed.

## Driving

When you use your own car for business, claim 0.47 GBP per mile for business miles. Do not claim for your normal commute. Pool cars from the Felixstowe depot should be used for trips over 150 miles where one is available.

## Daily allowance and hotel caps

The daily allowance covers meals, drinks and small incidental costs for each full day away. Claim the allowance for the city where you stay overnight. The hotel cap is the most the company pays for one night, including breakfast and taxes. Amounts are in GBP.

## Claiming expenses

Submit claims in the expenses system within 45 days of the cost. Attach an itemised receipt for any single cost over 25 GBP. Claims with a total over 750 GBP need approval from the Finance Controller as well as your line manager. Late claims are paid only with Finance Controller approval.

## Costs we do not reimburse

The company does not pay for alcohol beyond one drink with an evening meal, personal entertainment, fines and parking penalties, upgrades bought for personal reasons, or travel insurance bought separately, because the company policy already covers business trips.

## Corporate cards

Employees who travel more than six times a year may apply for a corporate card. Reconcile card spending in the expenses system by the fifth working day of each month.
