# Information security policy 2023

This policy applies from 1 May 2023 to every employee, contractor and agency worker who uses a Larkspur device, account or system. Status note: superseded by version 4.0 on 1 March 2026.

## Why this policy exists

Larkspur handles customer shipment data, customs records and payment details. A single compromised account can stop depot operations. This policy sets the minimum rules; the IT Security Lead may set stricter rules for specific systems.

## Accounts and passwords

Passwords for Larkspur accounts must be at least 10 characters long. Passwords must include an upper-case letter, a number and a symbol and must be changed every 90 days. Multi-factor authentication is required for email and remote access. An account locks after 5 failed sign-in attempts and the service desk unlocks it after checking your identity.

## Devices

Laptops have full disk encryption and are managed by IT. Your screen must lock automatically after 10 minutes without activity, and you should lock it yourself whenever you leave your desk. USB storage may be used for work files if the files are deleted after use. Personal phones may access company email after IT has enrolled them.

## Updates

Critical security updates must be installed within 7 days of release. Restart your laptop when prompted.

## Phishing and incidents

Report any suspicious email with the Report Phish button in the mail client within 4 hours of receiving it. Report a lost or stolen device to the IT service desk as soon as you notice, and in any case on the same day. Do not try to investigate an incident yourself.

## Remote access and networks

Use the company VPN to reach internal systems from outside a Larkspur site. Public Wi-Fi in hotels, ports and cafes is allowed only with the VPN switched on. Depot scanners and yard tablets connect only to the operations network and must never be joined to guest networks. Visitors use the guest network, which has no route to internal systems.

## Software

Install software only from the company software centre. If you need a tool that is not listed, raise a request with the IT service desk and explain the business need; the IT Security Lead checks licence terms and data handling before approval. Browser extensions follow the same rule. Unapproved software found during monthly scans is removed without notice.

## Data classification

All information is labelled Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer shipment manifests and customs records are Confidential. Payroll and health records are Restricted and may be shared only through the HR system. Never paste Confidential or Restricted data into public websites or unapproved online tools.

## Compliance

Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action. The IT Security Lead reviews the policy every year.
