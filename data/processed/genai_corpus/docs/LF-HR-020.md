# New starter onboarding guide

Welcome to Larkspur Freight Ltd (fictional). This guide explains what happens before you start and in your first months, so that you know who to ask and what to complete.

## Before your first day

Your line manager requests your system access at least 5 working days before your start date. HR sends your contract, the employee handbook and a short form for payroll and emergency contacts through the HR portal.

## Your first day

Collect your laptop from the IT desk at 09:30 on your first day. IT will help you set up multi-factor authentication and your first password, which must follow the information security policy. Your manager will introduce you to your team and to your buddy, an experienced colleague who helps you settle in. You and your buddy meet at least 4 times during your first month.

## Mandatory training

Complete the security awareness course in the learning portal within 10 working days of your start date. Complete the Code of Conduct e-learning within 30 days of your start date. Depot and warehouse starters also complete manual handling training before their first shift on site.

## Your first weeks

In week 2 every new starter, including office staff, spends a day at the Felixstowe depot to see how freight moves through the business. Your manager agrees three objectives with you by the end of your first month.

## Probation

The probation period is 6 months. Your manager holds review meetings at the end of months one, three and five, and confirms the outcome in writing before the end of probation. Probation may be extended once, by up to three months.

## Pay and benefits

Salaries are paid monthly on the 28th day of the month, or on the previous working day if the 28th falls at a weekend or on a public holiday. If you start after the payroll cut-off, your first payment includes the days worked in the previous month. Benefits enrolment opens in the HR portal after your first full month.

## Facilities and safety

On your first day at any site, a fire warden shows you the exits, assembly point and first aid stations. High-visibility vests and safety boots are required in all yard and warehouse areas, including for office staff on the depot day. Visitor badges are not valid for employees; your photo pass is printed in your first week. Report every accident or near miss in the safety app, even when nobody is hurt.

## Who to contact

For access and equipment, contact the IT service desk through the self-service portal. For pay, leave and contracts, contact HR through the HR portal. For anything else, ask your buddy or your line manager first.
